River Raid - Picocomputer 6502 port with native original game rules.
Original game: Activision (1982), designer Carol Shaw.
Reference disassembly and annotations: Thomas Jentzsch.

Run:
load riverraid.rp6502 [/auto | /psg | /opl2 | /pcm]

Sound options:
No option: OPL2. /auto: PCM if supported, otherwise OPL2.
Only /pcm requires the PCM firmware extension. /help shows usage.

Keyboard:
Player 1: arrows, Space/Enter. Player 2: W A S D, left Ctrl.
In single-player mode, both keyboard layouts work.
F1: color/B&W.                       F2: select GAME 1 / 2 / 3.
F3: start at bridge 1->5->20->50->1. F4: no function.
F5 / F6: Player 1 / 2 fire&forget or guided missiles.
Space or Enter: fire / start.        P: pause.
R: restart at selected bridge.       Escape: farewell screen, then exit.
Two gamepads: D-pad or left stick, face buttons to fire,
Start to pause, Select to restart.
GAME 3 DUO: simultaneous P1 (keyboard) and P2 (first gamepad or WASD).
Separate scores, fuel and lives. Shared scrolling averages both speeds.
Up to 10 lives per pilot, shown as two rows of five planes beside the fuel.
Faster planes gain distance over time, up to four plane heights (72 pixels).
Equal speeds preserve the earned lead; bullets are harmless
to your partner, but colliding planes lose a life each.
Wait for the next bridge to pass to rejoin, if you have a spare life.
Losing both planes restarts the current stage, keeping scores and lives.
Each lost plane costs one life. Game over when both pilots have no lives.
R starts a new game.
Fly over fuel tanks to re fuel. Destroy the bridge to finish a section.
Scoring:
Ship 30pts, Helicopter 60pts, Balloon 60pts, Fuel 80pts,
Road Tank 250pts, Bridge 500pts, Bridge with Tank 750pts.
