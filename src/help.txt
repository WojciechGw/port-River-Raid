River Raid - Picocomputer 6502 port with native original game rules.
Original game: Activision (1982), designer Carol Shaw.
Reference disassembly and annotations: Thomas Jentzsch.
The scrolling caption and attract demo start together after 3 seconds.

Player 1: arrows, Space/Enter. Player 2: WASD, left Ctrl.
In single-player mode, both keyboard layouts work.
F1: color/B&W. F2: select one/two players.
F3: start at BRIDGE 1 -> 5 -> 20 -> 50 -> 1.
F5/F4: toggle guided/straight missiles for player 1/2.
Space or Enter: fire / start. P: pause. R: restart at selected bridge. Escape: exit.
Two gamepads: D-pad or left stick, face buttons to fire,
Start to pause, Select to restart.
Fly over fuel tanks to refuel. Destroy the bridge to finish a section.
Balloon: 60 points. Road tank: 250. Bridge: 500. Bridge with tank: 750.
